Ambient Video
YouTube or any video site — with the glow around the picture
YouTube TV: sign in with your phone (it shows a code for yt.be/activate). Arrows, Enter and Esc move around it.
Paste any link (Plex, Vimeo, archive.org…) to open it here with the glow. Glow settings: the round button on the video, or ⌘, · ⌘G glow on/off · ⌘⇧H home · ⌃⌘F full screen.
Netflix, HBO and other protected services can't play here.
