Ambient Video – Privacy

Ambient Video (the website, the Chrome extension, the Mac app and the Pocket TV app) does not collect, store or send any personal data.

  • The glow is made on your own device from the colours at the edge of the video. Frames never leave your device.
  • Your glow settings (on/off, strength, video size) and recent links are saved only on your device or in your own Chrome sync storage.
  • There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.
