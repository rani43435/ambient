Help

Questions.

How does YouTube TV sign in?

It shows a code. Open yt.be/activate on your phone and type it in. In the browser, YouTube TV needs the Chrome extension.

Which links can I play?

YouTube and Vimeo play right here with the glow (Chrome will ask to share this tab). Any other link, like Plex or archive.org, opens in a new tab; then press Connect glow.

Does it work with Netflix or HBO?

No. Protected services block other pages from reading their picture, so the glow can't see the colours.

Is my data collected?

No. Everything runs on your device; recent links stay on your device. Read the privacy policy.
