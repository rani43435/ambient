How it works

A few things worth knowing.

How does YouTube TV sign in?

It shows a code — open yt.be/activate on your phone and type it in. YouTube TV here needs the Ambient Video Chrome extension.

Which links can I play?

YouTube and Vimeo play right here with the glow (Chrome on a computer will ask to share this tab). Any other link — Plex, archive.org… — opens in a new tab, then press Connect glow.

Does it work with Netflix or HBO?

No. Protected services block other pages from reading their picture, so the glow can't see the colours.

Is my data collected?

No. Everything runs in your browser; recent links stay on your device. Read the privacy policy.
